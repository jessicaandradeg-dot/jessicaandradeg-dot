## 🚀 Key Projects & Portfolio Highlight

### 🏪 [1. ATM Cash Replenishment Optimization & Cash-Mix Allocation](https://github.com/jessicaandradeg-dot/atm-cash-optimization)
* **Stack:** Python, PuLP (MILP with CBC & HiGHS Solvers), Monte Carlo Simulation, SciPy, Matplotlib.
* **Business Impact:** Solves the cash holding vs. stockout trade-off across ATM networks using Mixed-Integer Linear Programming (MILP). Models multi-denomination cassette distribution (R$ 20, R$ 50, R$ 100), opportunity cost of capital, and transport logistics. Evaluates performance against operational baselines (*Highest Denomination*, *Proportional*, *Threshold*) and provides audit logging per transaction.

### 💳 [2. Credit Risk Modeling with Cost-Sensitive LightGBM & MLOps Pipeline](https://github.com/jessicaandradeg-dot/credit-risk-lightgbm-mlops)
* **Stack:** Python, LightGBM, Optuna, MLflow, Pytest, Scikit-Learn.
* **Business Impact:** Mitigates asymmetric financial risk in retail credit by pairing Optuna hyperparameter search with a custom Cost-Matrix evaluator, shifting optimization from raw accuracy to net BRL loss minimization ($\text{FN} = \text{R}\$ 5.000$ vs $\text{FP} = \text{R}\$ 500$). Features automated unit tests and experiment tracking.

### 🧬 [3. Causal Inference in Public Health: Prenatal Care & Low Birth Weight](https://github.com/jessicaandradeg-dot/causal-birthweight-analysis)
* **Stack:** Python, Doubly Robust Estimation (AIPW), Non-Parametric Bootstrap (95% CI), PSM, IPW, Pytest.
* **Methodology:** Estimates the Average Treatment Effect (ATE) of preventive prenatal care on low birth weight by adjusting for confounding variables. Combines propensity score weighting with outcome regression via **Doubly Robust (AIPW)** estimators, computing exact 95% Confidence Intervals using **Bootstrap resampling**.

### 📊 [4. Credit Portfolio Analytics & Cohort Risk (SQL)](https://github.com/jessicaandradeg-dot/credit-cohort-analytics-sql)
* **Stack:** PostgreSQL, ANSI SQL, Window Functions, CTEs, Cohort Analysis.
* **Analytical Focus:** Engineered vintage cohort analysis for $90+$ DPD default evolution tracking, transition roll-rate matrices across risk buckets, and regulatory Expected Loss (PDD) calculations using EAD, PD, and LGD parameters.
