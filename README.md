# Hi, I'm Jéssica Andrade 👋

### Biomedical Engineering Ph.D. Candidate at COPPE/UFRJ | Applied Data Science, Machine Learning & Operations Research

I am a biomedical engineer and researcher with an M.Sc. in Biomedical Engineering and ongoing Ph.D. studies at COPPE/UFRJ. My work combines statistical modeling, machine learning, causal inference, and mathematical optimization to investigate decision problems in public health, finance, and operations.

I build reproducible analytical prototypes in Python, SQL, and R, with attention to evaluation, documentation, and methodological limitations. Portfolio experiments that use generated data are labeled as synthetic and should not be interpreted as evidence of real-world impact or production experience.

## 🚀 Selected Projects

### 💳 [Credit Pricing & Decision Engine](https://github.com/jessicaandradeg-dot/credit-pricing-decision-engine)
**Python · scikit-learn · Logistic Regression · MLP · Bootstrap · pytest**

Synthetic credit-risk and pricing prototype comparing fixed-rate and risk-aware policies. Includes a logistic-regression baseline, an MLP model comparison, a randomized two-rate experiment with ITT estimates and bootstrap confidence intervals, and sensitivity analysis for assumed recovery rates. All results are generated by a toy simulator; the project is not a lending or collections system.

### 👥 [Fair People Analytics Ranking](https://github.com/jessicaandradeg-dot/fair-people-analytics-ranking)
**Python · Ranking Metrics · Fairness Audit · Explainability · Drift**

Synthetic ranking prototype covering data integration, EDA, Precision@k/Recall@k/NDCG@k, group-level selection audits, a post-processing mitigation exercise, linear-model explanations, PSI drift checks, and an offline randomized pilot simulation. Not for screening or decisions about real people.

### 🏧 [ATM Cash Replenishment Optimization](https://github.com/jessicaandradeg-dot/atm-cash-optimization)
**Python · MILP · PuLP · CBC/HiGHS · Monte Carlo**

Operations research prototype for replenishment and cash-denomination allocation under uncertain demand. Compares an optimization model with heuristic policies using service-level and cost-oriented metrics in modeled scenarios.

### 🧬 [Causal Inference: Prenatal Care & Birth Weight](https://github.com/jessicaandradeg-dot/causal-birthweight-analysis)
**Python · PSM · IPW · AIPW · Bootstrap**

Study-style workflow for estimating treatment effects while adjusting for observed confounders, comparing propensity-score and doubly robust estimators, and examining uncertainty. Interpret results in light of the data-generating assumptions documented in the repository.

### 📊 [Credit Portfolio Analytics & Cohort Risk](https://github.com/jessicaandradeg-dot/credit-cohort-analytics-sql)
**PostgreSQL · SQL · CTEs · Window Functions**

SQL analyses for credit vintages, delinquency-bucket migration (roll rates), and expected-loss calculations using EAD, PD, and LGD.

### 📉 [Cost-Sensitive Credit Risk Modeling](https://github.com/jessicaandradeg-dot/credit-risk-lightgbm-mlops)
**Python · LightGBM · Optuna · MLflow · pytest**

Synthetic credit-risk pipeline with feature engineering, hyperparameter tuning, threshold selection using an asymmetric cost matrix, experiment tracking, and automated tests.

### 🔎 [Event Correlation & Root Cause Analysis](https://github.com/jessicaandradeg-dot/Event-Correlation-Root-Cause-Analysis)
**Python · Pandas · scikit-learn · Random Forest · Top-K Ranking**

Reproducible prototype for correlating synthetic system events and ranking likely root causes from temporal and severity signals. The repository discusses the gap between controlled synthetic data and production observability.
